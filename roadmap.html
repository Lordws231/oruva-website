Roadmap

From Osun, to Nigeria, to Africa.

  1. Now — Platform built, pilot in OsunThe booking platform is built and running in live staging; field-testing across 5+ cities and towns (Osogbo, Ile-Ife, Ilesa, Ede and more); shared rides live within Osun at pilot scope; Oruva Points live — Season 1 opens 26 October 2026.
  2. Next — Beachhead expansionLagos, Oyo and Abuja launch; fleet partnerships, driver finance at scale, Entertainment Hub phases and shared rides network-wide.
  3. Then — Nationwide NigeriaState-by-state rollout, mobile cinema and events network, business advertising marketplace, deeper ORVA governance.
  4. Vision — Across AfricaA pan-African organised mobility ecosystem, with African entertainment and community at its heart.
