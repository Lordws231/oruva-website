Why Oruva

What is broken about moving in Nigeria — and the one organised platform answering it.

Problem & Solution

The problem is real. So is our answer.

The problem today

  • Unpredictable fares — prices change by mood, weather and bargaining power.
  • Safety gaps — unverified drivers, poorly maintained vehicles, no accountability.
  • Fragmentation — informal operators in silos; no single trusted system.
  • Address failure — map pins fail where streets have no names; riders and drivers miss each other.
  • Exclusion — small towns, low-bandwidth areas and the unbanked are left out.
  • Drivers trapped — no access to loans, savings or vehicle finance; earnings leak away.
  • Dead time & missed trade — journeys are wasted time; local businesses cannot reach moving customers.

The Oruva solution

  • Upfront, transparent fares — know the price before the trip starts.
  • Verified, trained drivers — tiered verification, safety features and two-way ratings.
  • One organised platform — rides, shared rides, pickup & delivery, moving & haulage, fleets and routes, managed together.
  • Landmark-based pickups — “near the main gate” beats a wrong pin, every time.
  • Built for real conditions — low-bandwidth and offline-friendly flows; fiat and ORVA payments.
  • Driver finance built in — loan partnerships, savings plans for repairs, and token rewards.
  • Every journey works harder — entertainment, advertising and commerce woven into the trip.

Bridging the Gap

The gaps everyone else left out are exactly where Oruva begins.

The town gap

Global apps cluster in rich city centres. Oruva starts in Osun's cities and towns — Osogbo, Ile-Ife, Ilesa, Ede and underserved communities — and grows with field data from the ground, through Lagos, Oyo and Abuja, then nationwide and across Africa.

The trust gap

Between informal transport people rely on and formal platforms they don't. Verified drivers, community mapping and local context turn everyday trust into platform trust.

The money gap

Between cash, bank and crypto. Fiat and ORVA rails side by side mean no rider or driver is locked out by how they hold money — and drivers gain a path to loans and savings.

The opportunity gap

Between big platforms and the small retailer on the roadside. Oruva's advertising and commerce layer lets a roadside business reach moving customers like a big brand can.

The connection gap

A ride is treated as a transaction elsewhere. At Oruva it is a connection — rider networking, shared journeys and community that continues after drop-off.

The time gap

Hours spent in transit are dead time on other platforms. Oruva turns them into entertainment, discovery and earning time.
