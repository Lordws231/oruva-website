Features

Everything the best platforms do — plus everything they don't.

Oruva brings together the strengths riders love in platforms like Uber, Bolt, inDrive and Lagride — reliability, affordability, fare negotiation culture and scheduled professionalism — and closes the gaps every one of them leaves behind.

◈

Smart ride booking

Landmark-based pickups, negotiated fares agreed before the trip, route previews and pickup notes written the way people actually describe places — ride now, or book a driver or biker ahead for later.

👥Live across Osun

Shared rides

Split the fare, not the comfort. Oruva Shared matches riders going the same way with permanent Share Ride codes — cutting cost per trip, traffic and emissions. Shared rides run within Osun State today for Tier 3-verified members; interstate shared rides are coming soon.

🛡

Safety by design

Verified and trained drivers, Tier 1/2/3 identity verification, trip sharing, safety tools and mutual 5-star ratings — riders and drivers rate each other after every ride.

📦Coming Soon

Moving & haulage

Household goods, luggage and furniture moved through the same trusted platform — no informal guesswork. The Moving & Haulage section is built in the app and opens soon; register your interest there.

🛵

Pickup & delivery

Goods picked up and delivered on the right wheels for the job — bicycles, bikes and cars. Food arrives hot and parcels beat the traffic, businesses and services deliver further and grow — and every tier of transport, right down to the bicycle, becomes a way to earn.

📶

Works where signal is weak

Low-bandwidth and offline-friendly flows designed for real Nigerian conditions — not just the city centre at full bars.

🎮

Instant games — online & offline

A gaming section built into the platform: no downloads, no installs, just tap and play. Riders play online with friends, or offline on the road — every ride and quiet moment becomes fun, relaxing leisure.

🚗

Fleet & route management

Tools for fleet owners and transport companies to organise vehicles, drivers and routes inside one system.

💳

Pay your way

Paystack-powered card and bank payments today — drivers are settled directly by split payment. ORVA and blockchain payments arrive with the Blockchain Hub, coming soon.

🏦Coming Soon

Vehicle finance & savings

A Vehicle Finance section to help drivers get, own or upgrade their vehicles, and a Savings Mode for setting money aside inside the app. Both are built as in-app sections and open soon — no rates, no promises; just register your interest.

⭐

Rewards everywhere

Referrals, in-app rewards and Oruva Points that convert into ORVA — loyalty that pays the community back.

Only on Oruva

Built different — on purpose.

The platform is built and in live staging. These are the ideas that make Oruva Oruva — published numbers instead of fine print, and new ways to ride that are opening next, each labelled honestly.

⚖️

The Fare-Honesty Pledge

Our fare basis is published, not hidden: car rides start from a ₦1,500 peg for the first 1 km / 5 minutes, bike rides from ₦700 — then rider and driver negotiate the fare, and the full breakdown is shown before you confirm. No surge. Ever. The price agreed is the price paid.

💰

Drivers keep more

One published number: a 10% all-in platform fee — against the 20–30% taken elsewhere. Nothing else is taken from the fare. And if a rider tips, the tip is the driver's in full: optional post-ride tipping is built in, and 100% of every tip goes to the driver.

🗣Coming Soon

Book Your Way

Booking without the app, in development now: send a WhatsApp voice note — in Yoruba or Pidgin — and have your ride arranged from it; book by SMS from any phone; or place a missed call and Oruva calls you back to take the booking.

🛣Coming Soon

Corridor Shared Rides

Scheduled shared rides on the inter-town corridors people already travel — Osogbo–Ibadan, Ife–Osogbo and Ilesa–Akure — with advance booking, known pickup points and the fare agreed upfront, working with the motor parks along each route.

👨‍👩‍👧Coming Soon

Family Accounts

Fund rides for a family member from your own wallet, follow their trip live as it happens, and get a notification the moment the trip completes. Everyone keeps their own account — a Family Account links the people you love, it never merges them.

🏛Coming Soon

Driver Council

A council of drivers in every town Oruva serves — a collective, organised voice into how Oruva runs locally. No council sits yet; the section is built in the app so drivers can register interest from day one.
